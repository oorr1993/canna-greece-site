Medical cannabis in Greece for visitors — the complete guide

Last updated: July 26, 2026 · Reading time: about 6 minutes · CanaFlight

The short version

Medical cannabis has been legal in Greece since 2017, and since February 2024 licensed pharmacies have been dispensing it in practice on a doctor's prescription. A cannabis licence from another country is not valid in Greece, and flying in with cannabis is strictly prohibited. The only lawful route for a visitor: a prescription from a doctor licensed in Greece — a process you can now complete online, and if the doctor approves, in up to an hour during working hours on the VIP track, without leaving your hotel. The prescription is valid at any licensed pharmacy in Greece for up to 30 days, and what you buy in Greece stays in Greece.

Is medical cannabis legal in Greece? What the law actually says

Greece regulated medical cannabis in legislation back in 2017, but in practice it took years for the market to find its feet. The real turning point came in February 2024, when licensed pharmacies across the country began dispensing locally produced medical cannabis products on a doctor's prescription. In other words: today it is not merely "legal on paper" — it works on the ground.

Three things are important to understand about the Greek framework:

  • Only on prescription. Using, possessing or buying without a doctor's prescription remains a criminal offence — for visitors too. There are no Amsterdam-style coffee shops in Greece.
  • The prescription must be Greek. A doctor licensed in Greece is the only person authorised to issue a prescription that local pharmacies will honour.
  • Visitors are eligible too. The law does not restrict prescriptions to Greek citizens — the medical assessment is made against a passport, and that is precisely what allows visitors to receive lawful treatment during their stay.

Can I fly into Greece with cannabis from home? (Spoiler: no)

Even if you hold a valid medical cannabis licence at home — it is a document of that country, and it confers no rights in Greece. Bringing cannabis into Greek territory without prior authorisation from the Greek National Organization for Medicines (EOF) is a drugs offence in every sense, and the risk starts at your departure airport and ends at customs in Athens.

The simple, lawful alternative: arrive "clean", and arrange a local prescription — either in advance from home, or on the first day of your trip.

How do you get a cannabis prescription in Greece? Three routes

1. An in-person appointment with a Greek doctor

Possible, but unrealistic for most visitors: you need to find a doctor who works with medical cannabis, get an appointment in high season, travel to the clinic in person, and get through the consultation in Greek or in medical English. That can burn a whole day of your trip — and sometimes several days of waiting.

2. An international digital service

There are online services that connect visitors with Greek doctors. They operate in English and are built for a general international audience — without familiarity with the specific needs of any one group of travellers, including the prohibition on taking product home.

Where does it work? Not just Athens

The prescription is digital and valid at any licensed pharmacy in the country — Athens, Thessaloniki, Crete, Rhodes, Kos, Santorini, Mykonos, Corfu, Zakynthos and Halkidiki. Even on a proper island-hopping trip, one prescription covers the whole route.

The golden rules: how not to get into trouble

  1. Use in private accommodation only — a hotel or a holiday apartment. Not on the beach, not in the street, not in a taverna.
  2. Zero driving under the influence — exactly as with alcohol, and Greek enforcement is not forgiving.
  3. Prescription and passport on you at all times whenever you buy or carry the product.
  4. Nothing flies home. Importing cannabis into your home country may well be a criminal offence — what is bought in Greece is finished in Greece.

Before you apply: what matters to raise with the doctor

Medical cannabis is not suitable for everyone, and the medical decision — including whether it is appropriate at all, the concentration and the dosage — rests with the licensed doctor alone, on the basis of the information you provide. It is important to raise any relevant information with the doctor, including:

  • The process is intended for those aged 18 and over only.
  • It is not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
  • Particular caution is required with a personal or family history of psychosis, schizophrenia or another mental health condition.
  • It may interact with other medication you take — tell the doctor about it.
  • Do not drive or operate machinery under the influence of cannabis.

The information in this section is general, is intended to draw your attention to matters worth raising with the doctor, and does not constitute medical advice or a substitute for it.

What is the difference between medical and recreational in Greece?

Only medical cannabis on prescription is lawful. Recreational use without a prescription remains an offence — which is why the prescription is not a formality, it is your legal protection.

Is the Greek prescription valid in other European countries?

No. The prescription is valid in Greece only, and as noted — taking products out of Greek borders is prohibited to any destination.
