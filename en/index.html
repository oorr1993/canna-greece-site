FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is medical cannabis legal in Greece?

Yes. Legal since 2017, and since February 2024 licensed pharmacies dispense medical cannabis products with a doctor's prescription. Use without a prescription remains illegal — the prescription is exactly what we arrange. Official source: the Greek National Organization for Medicines (EOF).

Do I need an existing cannabis license from my home country?

No. The process is entirely Greek. Even if you've never held a cannabis license anywhere, you may qualify — the Greek doctor makes a fresh, independent assessment based on your questionnaire.

How long does it take?

The questionnaire — about 3 minutes. The doctor's review — up to an hour during working hours on VIP, up to 24 hours on Fast, and one to two weeks on Basic. Tip: fill it in before your flight and land with a prescription ready.

I don't have a "serious illness" — do I stand a chance?

Yes. The criteria in Greece are broader than in many countries: chronic pain, back pain, insomnia, anxiety and migraines are all considered. The final decision always rests with the doctor. Not approved? Full refund.

Can I fly into Greece with my own cannabis?

Strongly not recommended. Foreign licenses and prescriptions are not valid in Greece, and importing cannabis without prior authorization from the Greek regulator is prohibited. The legal, simple path: a local prescription from a licensed Greek doctor — that's us.

Can I take products back home after the trip?

No. Flat no. The prescription and the products are legal in Greece only — taking cannabis across borders is a criminal offense. What's bought in Greece, stays in Greece.
